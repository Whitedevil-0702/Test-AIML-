# Assessment 01 — AI Concepts and Problem Framing

**Difficulty:** Beginner  
**Deliverables:** `concept-map.md` and `problem-framing.md`

## Task A — Concept map (40 points)

Create a diagram showing AI, ML, deep learning, and generative AI. Include:
- Plain-language definitions.
- Two examples.
- One misconception you corrected.

## Task B — Problem framing (40 points)

Pick one problem and state:
- User and use case.
- Desired output.
- Whether rules, ML, or another approach is a sensible starting point.
- Data needed and possible limitations.
- A measurable success criterion.

## Task C — Explain and reflect (20 points)

Explain the difference between training and inference in your own words. Identify one risk or limitation.

## Review rubric

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Concept distinctions are accurate | 20 |
| Examples and diagram are understandable | 20 |
| Problem framing and method choice are justified | 25 |
| Data, metric, and limitation are identified | 20 |
| Clear writing and reflection | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Completion:** Suggested threshold 70/100, all requested files present, and review feedback addressed.
